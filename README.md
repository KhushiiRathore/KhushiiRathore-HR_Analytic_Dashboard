# HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI Project)
🎥 Project Demo Video: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zJBgRQAM4KsZCHPhf8kOJWbeldve10Hh/view?usp=sharing
## Objective
To analyze employee attrition and identify key factors affecting employee turnover across departments.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query (data cleaning)
- DAX (measures and KPIs)

## Features
- Department and Gender slicers
- KPIs: Employee Count, Attrition, Attrition Rate, Avg Age, Salary, Tenure
- Attrition analysis by Age, Salary, Education, Job Role, Years at Company

## Key Insights
- Highest attrition occurs in age group 26–35
- Lower salary bands show higher attrition
- Sales and R&D departments contribute most to attrition
- Attrition is highest in first few years of employment
